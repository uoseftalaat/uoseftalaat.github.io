# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

The Architectural Atelier of Quality Engineering — ground of warm Japanese paper (#F5F1EB) juxtaposed with sumi ink charcoal (#1C1A17) and lacquer cinnabar vermilion (#C0392B). Alternates with atmospheric midnight gallery sections (#121417) for high-impact metric revelations.

## Typography

- Display: Reem Kufi & Playfair Display / Amiri (monumental weight, negative tracking -0.025em, tight leading)
- Editorial: Amiri (classic literary serif)
- Body & Interface: Tajawal (geometric humanist sans with crystal clarity)
- Code & Measurement: JetBrains Mono (fixed-width tabular clarity)

## Palette

- Background: #F5F1EB (warm paper)
- Surface: #FFFFFF (crisp white card)
- Surface Dark: #1C1E24 (midnight gallery card)
- Ink Dark: #121417 (deep midnight backdrop)
- Ink Text: #1C1A17 (primary AAA ink)
- Muted: #7A7367 (secondary editorial)
- Accent: #C0392B (cinnabar lacquer vermilion)
- Antique Gold: #855C00 (verified credentials & badges)
- Border: #DDD8CF (warm stone hairline)

## Motion & Depth

- Apple fluid motion: critically damped springs (damping 1.0, response 0.35s).
- Direct pointer-down compression (scale 0.975) without artificial delay.
- Translucent chrome with backdrop-filter blur(16px).
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-reduced-transparency.
